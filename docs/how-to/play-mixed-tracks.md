# Play Mixed Tracks

Learn how to play, mix, and monitor separated tracks in real-time.

## Multi-Track Playback

Descombinator Pro plays **Vocals** and **Instrumental** tracks in sync. Each track has independent controls.

### Playback Controls

| Control | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Play/Pause | Start or pause playback |
| Stop | Stop playback and reset position |
| Seek slider | Drag to seek to a position |
| Volume slider | Adjust track volume |
| Mute toggle | Mute/unmute track |

### Per-Track Mixing

- **Volume:** Adjust the volume of each track independently
- **Mute:** Toggle mute on/off for each track
- **Solo:** Listen to a single track by muting all others

## Waveform Visualization

The waveform view shows the audio waveform with a playback position indicator.

- The position line updates in real-time during playback
- Click on the waveform to seek to that position
- Zoom in/out to see more or less detail

## Gapless Playback

Gapless playback ensures seamless looping and transitions between tracks. This is especially useful for DJing or continuous playback.

## Tips

- Use the **mute** button to isolate a track and hear it clearly
- Adjust **volume** to balance vocals and instrumental
- Click on the **waveform** to quickly navigate to a specific section
- Use **gapless playback** for continuous listening
